## Publishing Tips

### For Pinterest Creator Dashboard:
1. Go to [Pinterest Pin Creation](https://www.pinterest.com/pin/create)
2. Upload image from `images/` folder
3. Paste title from CSV (max 100 chars)
4. Paste description from CSV (max 500 chars)
5. Add board: "Plant-Based Living" or "Healthy Recipes"
6. Schedule for date/time from CSV
7. Repeat for all 29 pins

### For OneSignal Journey:
1. Create journey in OneSignal
2. Import CSV data
3. Set up Pinterest integration
4. Schedule journey starting May 8, 2026
5. OneSignal will publish pins automatically every 3 days

### Expected Performance:
- **Week 1:** 5-10% of pins will have impressions
- **Month 1:** 20-30% of pins accumulate saves
- **Month 2:** Content gains momentum, repins increase
- **Month 3:** Long-tail traffic continues, back-catalog grows
- **6+ months:** Evergreen content drives consistent traffic
