## Title & Description Strategy

### Titles (100 chars max)
✅ Action-oriented language ("Learn," "Discover," "Complete Guide")  
✅ Specific benefits highlighted (e.g., "Creamy Foam," "No Bitterness")  
✅ Problem-solution format where relevant  
✅ Searchable keywords (e.g., "Plant Milk," "Best," "Recipe")  
✅ Curiosity hooks for comparisons (e.g., "vs," "Taste Test")

**Examples:**
- "Berry Smoothie Bowl with Plant Milk - Easy 5-Minute Recipe"
- "Best Plant Milk for Cappuccino: Creamy Foam Latte Art Guide"
- "Oat Milk vs Almond Milk: Full Nutrition & Taste Comparison"

### Descriptions (500 chars max)
✅ Lead with the biggest benefit immediately  
✅ Specific, not generic ("which milk creates silky microfoam" vs "learn about milk")  
✅ Include searchable keywords naturally  
✅ Mention what's included (guides, tips, recipes, comparisons)  
✅ Address reader pain points  
✅ Call to action implied (click to read full article)

**Example:**
> "Learn which plant milk froths like dairy for perfect cappuccinos at home. Barista-tested guide shows which milk creates silky microfoam and beautiful latte art. Steaming tips included."
